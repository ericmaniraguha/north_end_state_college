Our Story

Learn today, lead tomorrow

North End College is a college in Lusaka, Zambia, located at Findeco House. We offer full-time diploma programmes in business and management, education, social sciences and public administration, and media and communication.

Our approach combines academic excellence with practical learning. Students are taught by qualified lecturers, supported throughout their studies, and guided towards careers and further study.
