FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How do I apply?

Apply using the online form on this page, or send your Grade 12 results and NRC to our admissions team on WhatsApp (+260 771 413 910 or +260 977 494 707).

What are the entry requirements?

You need a Grade 12 certificate or results and a National Registration Card (NRC).

When does the next intake start?

The next intake is January 2027, for full-time study.

How quickly will I hear back?

Once your documents are verified, you'll receive an instant acceptance letter.

How much are the fees?

Please contact admissions by phone or WhatsApp for the current fees for your programme.

Where is the campus?

Our campus is at Findeco House, Floor No. 17, Lusaka, Zambia. See the map.
